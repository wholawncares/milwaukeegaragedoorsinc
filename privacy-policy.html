Privacy and data

Milwaukee Garage Doors Inc

Information We Collect

This website does not include a contact form that sends information anywhere. If you call Milwaukee Garage Doors Inc at (224) 842-7761, the company receives the phone number you call from and whatever details you share about the garage door and the service address.

No other information collection is described on this page. This website does not collect payment information.

Like most websites, these pages are delivered over the internet, and standard technical information, such as the page requested, is handled by the web server and the visitor's browser. No tracking or analytics practice is described on this page.

How We Use Information

Information shared by phone is used to respond to the call and to arrange the garage door work discussed.

Details shared about the door and the address help the company understand the work before any visit.

Questions about how information is used can be asked by phone at (224) 842-7761.

Cookies and Tracking

No cookie or tracking practice is described on this page. Your browser's cookie and privacy controls apply to any cookies your browser stores.

These pages load fonts from a third-party font service. Your browser handles that request under its own privacy controls.

Analytics and Third-Party Tools

No analytics tool and no third-party scheduling or communication tool is described on this page.

The only third-party content loaded by these pages is the font service named above and, on the homepage, an embedded map. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

The company does not describe selling personal information on this page.

Information may be disclosed if required by law, such as in response to a court order, or to protect the safety and rights of customers, the team, or the public.

Data Retention

Information shared by phone is kept only as long as needed for the work discussed and for business records.

If you ask for information to be removed and the company is not required to keep it, it will be deleted from active records. Ask by calling (224) 842-7761.

Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information shared with the company from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, and access is limited to those who need it to serve you.

No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

User Rights

You may ask what personal information the company holds about you, ask for a copy, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

To make a request, call Milwaukee Garage Doors Inc at (224) 842-7761. The company will respond and help with the request.

Children's Privacy

This website and these services are meant for adults. The company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared information, call (224) 842-7761 so it can be removed.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practices or for legal reasons. When it is updated, the new version will be posted on this page.
