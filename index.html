Problems we solve

Common Garage Door Problems in Zion

Garage door failures tend to fall into a few patterns. The four below cover the main patterns, in general terms.

01

A torsion spring snapped and the door will not lift

A loud bang, a door that feels heavy, or a visible gap in the coil can mean a broken spring. Spring work is under high tension and is framed throughout this site as technician-only work. See also: Garage door spring replacement.

02

The door is off track and hanging crooked

A door that has jumped its track, or hangs unevenly, should not be forced, and the opener should not keep running it. Cables, rollers, tracks, and hinges are checked together, since they fail together. See also: Garage door off-track, cable and roller repair.

03

The opener hums but the door does not move

A humming opener with a still door can mean a problem in the opener, or a broken spring fighting the motor. The opener and the door are checked separately so the fault is found in the right system. See also: Garage door opener repair and installation.

04

The door closes, then reverses

A door that reverses as it closes can mean misaligned or dirty safety sensors, or an auto-reverse response. Federal rules (16 CFR Part 1211, UL 325) require residential automatic garage door operators to have entrapment protection: an inherent reversal system plus a secondary entrapment protection device (such as photoelectric sensors or an edge sensor) or a constant-contact control. Test sensors and auto-reverse regularly. See also: Garage door opener repair and installation.
